Real estate agents write more marketing copy than almost any other profession — and most of it is terrible. "Charming 3BR in desirable neighborhood" isn't selling anything. It's filler, and buyers scroll right past it.

AI tools like ChatGPT can write significantly better copy. But only if you know how to prompt them. A generic prompt gives you generic output. A real-estate-specific prompt gives you a listing description that makes buyers call before the open house.

Why Real Estate Agents Need Purpose-Built AI Prompts

Real estate marketing has unique constraints that generic AI doesn't understand:

  • Fair Housing compliance — AI can accidentally generate language that violates Fair Housing Act guidelines. Purpose-built prompts include guardrails.
  • MLS formatting — character limits, required fields, and conventions vary by market. Good prompts account for this.
  • Buyer vs. seller messaging — the content you write for sellers (listing presentations, market updates) is completely different from buyer-facing content (property descriptions, neighborhood guides).
  • Local market knowledge — prompts with fill-in variables for neighborhood names, school districts, and local landmarks produce far more compelling copy.

What You Can Create With Real Estate AI Prompts

Property Listing Descriptions

This is where most agents start — and where AI has the biggest impact. Instead of recycling the same 50 adjectives, AI prompts generate unique, compelling descriptions for each listing. Feed it the property details and get back copy that highlights the lifestyle, not just the features.

Example output: "Morning coffee on a wraparound porch with views of Maple Ridge. This renovated 4BR colonial sits on a half-acre corner lot in Westfield's top-rated school district. Original hardwood floors meet a completely modernized kitchen — because you shouldn't have to choose between character and convenience. Finished basement adds 600 sq ft of flex space. 8 minutes to downtown, 2 minutes to the rail."

Buyer & Seller Email Sequences

Nurturing leads is a volume game. AI prompts help you create multi-email drip sequences for new buyer leads, seller prospects, past clients, and sphere of influence. Each email sounds personal — not like it came from a CRM template from 2018.

Social Media Content

"Just listed" and "just sold" posts are table stakes. AI prompts help you create market update graphics, neighborhood spotlights, buyer tips, and personal brand content that positions you as the local expert — not just another agent posting house photos.

Open House Promotions

From Facebook event descriptions to Instagram stories copy to neighbor invitation letters, AI prompts cover the full open house marketing funnel. One prompt generates a complete multi-channel promotion package.

Client Communication

Offer follow-ups, inspection result explanations, closing checklists, and anniversary check-ins — all the touchpoints that build referral-worthy relationships. AI prompts make these effortless to maintain at scale.

5 Free Real Estate AI Prompts

  1. Listing Description: "Write a compelling MLS listing description (max 500 words) for a [bedrooms]BR/[bathrooms]BA [property type] in [neighborhood], [city]. Key features: [list features]. Highlight the lifestyle and neighborhood. Do NOT use: charming, nestled, boasts, stunning. Comply with Fair Housing language guidelines."
  2. New Buyer Lead Email: "Write a warm, non-pushy first email to a buyer lead who inquired about properties in [area]. Introduce yourself, mention one specific thing about the area that shows local knowledge, and suggest a quick call. Keep it under 150 words. Sign off as [Your Name], [Brokerage]."
  3. Market Update Post: "Write a LinkedIn post (200 words max) summarizing the [city/neighborhood] real estate market for [month/year]. Include: median price change, inventory levels, and one actionable insight for both buyers and sellers. Tone: authoritative but approachable."
  4. Open House Instagram Story: "Write 4 sequential Instagram story slides promoting an open house at [address] on [date/time]. Slide 1: hook/tease. Slide 2: key feature highlight. Slide 3: neighborhood/lifestyle angle. Slide 4: details + CTA. Each slide under 30 words."
  5. Seller Listing Presentation Email: "Write a follow-up email to a homeowner you met at a listing appointment for [address]. Reference 2 specific things about their home, include your suggested list price of [price], and explain one marketing strategy you'll use. Professional but confident tone."
